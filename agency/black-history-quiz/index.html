About This App

💡 About

This app celebrates Black History Month by making learning interactive and fun. Explore important moments, influential figures, and lasting contributions to society through quiz challenges and in-depth study.

🎮 Game Modes

Single Player: Test your knowledge with timed quizzes across three difficulty levels.

Study Mode: Browse fascinating facts by topic and dive deeper with AI-powered learning conversations.

Multiplayer (Coming Soon): Compete against others in real-time!

ℹ️ How Scoring Works

Each correct answer earns:

🟢 Easy: 10 points + time bonus
🟡 Medium: 20 points + time bonus
🔴 Hard: 30 points + time bonus

Time Bonus: Up to 6 extra points based on speed (Time Left ÷ 5)

Maximum Scores:

🟢 Easy: 80 points (5 × 16)
🟡 Medium: 130 points (5 × 26)
🔴 Hard: 180 points (5 × 36)

💡 All scores compete on one leaderboard - can you reach the top?
